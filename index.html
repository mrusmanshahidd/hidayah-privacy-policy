Overview

Hidayah provides Quran reading, a Printed Mushaf, Quran audio, prayer times, Qibla, Duas, Tasbeeh, the 99 Names, an Islamic Calendar, bookmarks, and related Islamic utilities. You do not need to create an account to use the app.

Hidayah does not sell personal information or use user data for advertising. The current Android app does not include an advertising or analytics service.

Location

Hidayah may request access to approximate or precise foreground location to provide prayer times and Qibla direction. It does not request background location. You can deny location permission and enter a city or coordinates manually.

Saved coordinates are used on your device to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. When the app needs a readable place name, it may pass coordinates to the device platform’s geocoding service. If you enter a city without coordinates, the platform geocoding service may process that city and country to find coordinates. The provider used by this service depends on your device.

If no usable location is saved or available, Hidayah may request an approximate city and coordinates from ipwho.is. That request sends your public IP address to the service; it does not send device GPS coordinates. The resulting approximate location can be saved on your device for prayer times and Qibla.

Notifications

Hidayah may request notification permission when you enable prayer reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device using your selected prayer settings and location. On supported Android versions, the app may also request exact-alarm access for precise reminders; when that access is unavailable, it can schedule approximate reminders.

Internet and third-party services

Internet access is used for Quran audio streaming and location-related network functions. The app’s normal Quran audio endpoint is hidayah-quran-audio.mrusmanshahidd.workers.dev. Audio requests include the selected reciter and verse; streaming may then connect to an HTTPS media URL returned by that service, including audio.qurancdn.com. These connections also expose your public IP address to the services involved as part of normal internet access.

  • ipwho.is: approximate location lookup based on your public IP address when no usable location is available.
  • Device platform geocoding: optional place-name lookup from coordinates or city lookup; the network provider depends on your device.
  • Quran audio service and media host: reciter and verse requests, followed by audio streaming when you use audio features.

The About screen also offers optional links to tanzil.net and sunnah.com. Those sites open only when you choose their links and have their own privacy practices. Quran text, translations, and Printed Mushaf PDFs are bundled with the app for offline reading.

Information stored on your device

Hidayah can save reading positions, bookmarks, favorites, Tasbeeh counts, selected reciter, reminder settings, prayer settings, saved location, and other app preferences locally on your device. Bundled Quran and Printed Mushaf content, Duas, and other local reading features can be used without an internet connection. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored app data, subject to your device’s backup and restore settings.

Children

Hidayah does not require an account or ask users to provide their age. Its location, notification, and audio features work as described in this policy. Parents and guardians can manage app permissions through the device’s settings.

Security

The app uses HTTPS for its direct requests to the Quran audio service and ipwho.is. Information saved locally is subject to your device’s security and backup settings. No method of storing or transmitting information is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to Hidayah or its services. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.
